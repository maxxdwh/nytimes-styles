A landmark gathering of business and political leaders that defined the year's most important conversations.

The 2021 DealBook Summit brought together CEOs, policymakers, entrepreneurs, and cultural figures for a day of wide-ranging conversations at Jazz at Lincoln Center in New York City.

From the future of finance and the global supply chain crisis to the metaverse and the future of work, the summit tackled the questions shaping our world.
