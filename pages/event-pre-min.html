Climate Hub 2022

October 3–7, 2022

New York City

Presented by Bloomberg Philanthropies

An immersive week of conversations, workshops, and activations exploring how we address the climate crisis.

Climate Hub is The New York Times's gathering place for world leaders, scientists, entrepreneurs, and advocates to share bold ideas and solutions for the defining challenge of our time.

Set against the backdrop of New York Climate Week, join us for free public programming across five days of panels, workshops, film screenings, and networking.

No ticket required for general admission to public programming. Space is limited — register to reserve your spot.

FAQ

  • Yes. All public programming at Climate Hub 2022 is free to attend.

  • Registration is encouraged but not required. Some sessions may have limited capacity.

  • Climate Hub takes place at Pier 17, 89 South Street, New York City.
