Climate Hub 2022

Al Gore

45th Vice President of the United States; Founder & Chairman, The Climate Reality Project

Al Gore served as the 45th Vice President of the United States from January 1993 to January 2001 under President Bill Clinton. He was the Democratic nominee for President in 2000.

Gore is the founder and chairman of The Climate Reality Project, a nonprofit devoted to solving the climate crisis. He is co-founder and chairman of Generation Investment Management and a partner at Kleiner Perkins. He currently serves on the board of directors of Apple Inc. and is a senior advisor to Google.

He was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 2007, along with the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change, for his work on climate change. His documentary "An Inconvenient Truth" won two Academy Awards.

Gore is the author of several books, including "Earth in the Balance," "An Inconvenient Truth," "The Assault on Reason," "Our Choice," "The Future," and "An Inconvenient Sequel: Truth to Power."
