Dealbook Summit 2021

The Future of Finance

November 9, 2021, 10:00–10:45 a.m. ET

Jazz at Lincoln Center, New York City

Watch the full session replay · 45 min

A wide-ranging conversation on the forces reshaping global finance — from crypto to central banks to the future of the dollar.

In the years since the 2008 financial crisis, the architecture of global finance has been remade. Central banks have become the most powerful actors in the global economy, cryptocurrencies have moved from the fringes to the mainstream, and a new generation of fintech companies has challenged the dominance of Wall Street.

Andrew Ross Sorkin sat down with some of the most influential figures in global finance to understand what comes next: higher interest rates, a potential recession, and the long-term future of digital assets.

This session explored the defining questions facing investors, policymakers, and anyone whose financial future depends on understanding where markets are headed.
